# Student Problem Map

VitalNotes should be organized around the problems paramedic students actually experience.

This map supports [[Where to Begin]], the future app navigation, and the learning path.

It should help students enter the guide through the problem they are actually having without turning VitalNotes into a quiz, diagnostic tool, or advice generator.

---

## Current Review Question

Do these problems reflect what paramedic students actually experience in labs, scenarios, OSCEs, and studying?

Current answer:

Yes. The problems are holding.

They reflect common student struggles without becoming too narrow or overly clinical.

The map should remain practical and student-facing.

Strong current coverage:

- studying but blanking during scenarios, at both the problem-explanation and recall-system level
- notes that do not help thinking
- facts that do not connect
- directive anxiety, at the understanding and recall-prompt level
- early interpretation of learning strain
- converting some scenario errors into recall prompts

Still needs future coverage:

- clinical reasoning under uncertainty
- safe pattern recognition
- scenario day learning

- focused practice after feedback

## Use in App Navigation

This map may later support:

- [[Where to Begin]]
- problem-based navigation cards
- Learning Path entry prompts
- related section suggestions

It should not become:

- a quiz
- a diagnostic tool
- a personalized algorithm
- a replacement for reading the guide
- a complex onboarding process

The student should be able to recognize their problem quickly and choose a useful place to begin.
