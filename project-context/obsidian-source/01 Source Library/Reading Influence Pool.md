# Reading Influence Pool

This note tracks books and texts that quietly shape the VitalNotes rebuild.

They are not here for name-dropping.

They are here to keep the project intellectually grounded while the student-facing voice stays practical, grounded, and useful.

The books and texts in this pool should shape structure, explanation, pacing, tool design, and review standards.

They should not dominate the student-facing content by name unless explicitly useful.

---

## Use Rule

Use these texts as background influence.

Do not turn VitalNotes into:

- a book summary project
- an academic literature review
- a productivity system
- a motivational reading list
- a citation-heavy guide
- a place where every influence needs to be visible

Student-facing sections should feel like they were written by an experienced paramedic instructor who understands learning, not by someone trying to prove they have read widely.
