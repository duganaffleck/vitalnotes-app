# Scenario Generator Influence

This note tracks ideas from the Scenario Generator project that should inform VitalNotes.

VitalNotes is student-facing.
Scenario Generator is instructor-facing.

They should feel related, but they are not the same product.

## Shared Principles

- Paramedic education should focus on reasoning, not checklist performance alone.
- Scenarios should expose thinking under pressure.
- Good learning is patient-centred and realistic.
- Assessment should be fair, defensible, and aligned to student level.
- Dynamic progression matters because real patients change.
- Reassessment is a reasoning act, not a ritual.
- Feedback should lead to a specific next adjustment.

## Scenario Generator Ideas That Belong in VitalNotes

- clinical reasoning under uncertainty
- dynamic patient progression
- appropriate care versus delayed or incorrect care
- common error patterns
- GRS-informed thinking without overwhelming students
- scenario days as learning tools
- OSCE preparation as pressure management
- reflection tied to future action

## Ideas to Keep Separate

- full simulation document generation
- detailed instructor setup logistics
- long GRS anchor generation
- backend JSON schemas
- full Ontario directive rule engines

## Relationship Between Products

Scenario Generator helps instructors build better practice.

VitalNotes helps students learn from practice.
